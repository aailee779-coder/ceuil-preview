Questions

Good to know.

When will the villas be delivered?

The project is scheduled for completion within 20 months from 1 September 2026, with final delivery expected by 1 May 2028.¹

When is the property title issued?

Title deeds are issued on completion of the structural works.¹

How are payments made?

In seven milestones tied to construction progress: 35% on reservation, then 15%, 10%, 10%, 15%, 10% and 5% at key handover.¹

What does every villa include?

A private garden, a private pool with jacuzzi and a hammam with a dedicated massage room. Selected villas also have a fully equipped basement with a private gym and games room.¹

Can I rent out my villa?

Yes. The estate offers a concierge and rental management service.¹

Why Ceuil?

Eleven private villas in a gated estate by Pons & Zaghloul, 5 km along the Route de Taghnaout. Each with its own garden, pool with jacuzzi and hammam, a 7-metre entrance gate and 8-metre ceilings. Around them: the Lussy Health Center, concierge, café room service, two full-time guards and 13 golf courses within 20 km.
